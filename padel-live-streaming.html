Instant Replays

Capture the padel moments people want to replay: glass saves, bandejas, viboras, smashes, long rallies and match points. Build quick replay sequences without stopping the stream.

  • Save rallies and winners while the match continues

  • Animated transitions and replay bumper

Branding & Sponsor Ads

Padel broadcasts are useful for sponsors because every match can carry club identity. Add logos, event branding and sponsor videos during replays, court changes or breaks between matches.

  • Club, league or tournament logo on screen
  • Sponsor videos during replays and court breaks

Remote Score Control

Mount the streaming iPhone once and let a second person update the padel score from another device. It is useful when the camera is high behind glass or away from the scorekeeper.

Picture-in-Picture

Keep the stream live while you check court schedules, send match updates or coordinate the next players. Picture-in-Picture keeps the broadcast visible while you handle club logistics.

Send padel matches to YouTube Live, Facebook Live, Twitch, a club website or any RTMP endpoint. HD 1080 output and adaptive bitrate help when indoor club Wi-Fi gets crowded.
